Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Nightmare Engine is a personal tool run on the channel owner's own computer. It signs in only to the owner's Google account.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Sheets and Google Drive files that belong to the owner (topic lists, scripts, video files).
  • The owner's YouTube channel: uploading the owner's videos, editing their titles and descriptions, and reading channel analytics.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to produce and publish the owner's own videos. It stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google account. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
